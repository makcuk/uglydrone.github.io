About

Vision

There is a massive gap between hobby drones that are affordable but limited, and professional or military-grade drones that are powerful but prohibitively expensive. Ugly Drone fills this gap with a field-configurable, ultra-low-cost platform that is modular, 3D-printable, and disposable if necessary.

Key Features

  • Field Printable & Assembled
    All structural parts are 3D-printable with basic filament, enabling local production in austere environments. No need to ship delicate airframes—just ship the files.
  • Highly Modular
    Designed as a stackable ecosystem. Frames, payload bays, AI computers, FPV cameras, batteries, and mission modules can be swapped in minutes.
  • Configurable Mission Profiles
    • Simple FPV Drone: Static payload, reconnaissance, or loutering munitions.
    • Payload Carrier: Drop-and-go missions for supply, mine fields creation or sensor deployment.
    • Autonomous/AI Drone: Companion computer or mission module adds autonomous targeting, following, or pre-programmed routes.
  • Extremely Affordable
    Base cost is so low that operators don't care if one is lost. A single unit is dramatically cheaper than a DJI, let alone military-grade solutions.
  • Boxy, Rugged Design
    Prioritizes ease of manufacture and repair over aesthetics—flat panels, simple mounts, and standardized connectors.
  • Portable Ecosystem
    Modules and airframes pack neatly into standard transport boxes for rapid deployment.
